# Dashboard

The home screen ([Dashboard](/)). Every figure comes from the same order maths as the Orders screen and is reconciled (Sales − Collected = To collect − Credit); if a check fails the screen shows a red "These figures don't add up" banner instead of numbers.

## Top: money to collect (all time, as of today)
- **To collect** — every order's unpaid remainder; cancelled and deleted orders excluded.
- **Age bar** — 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ days by order date; tap a band for [Dues](/dues) filtered to it.
- **Collect from** — top 5 customers, oldest dues first, with Call and WhatsApp reminder. **See all** opens [Dues](/dues).

## Period section
Chips **All time · This month · Last month · This FY · Custom…**; the exact dates show under them. Tiles: **Sales** (orders dated in the period), **Collected** (payments received in the period, including on older orders), **Kg sold** (+ pieces, ≈ kg from pieces), **Expenses** (bills dated in the period), each compared with the same-length period before. Charts: sales vs collected and kg sold for 6 months; top sizes; expenses by category. There is no profit figure on purpose: raw-material bills are used over many months.

## Why figures can differ
- Dashboard leaves out cancelled orders; the Orders list shows them (struck through).
- Collected leaves out payments on deleted orders and payments on the invoice itself; the ⓘ lists both when present.
- For the same figures, use the dues and period_summary tools.
